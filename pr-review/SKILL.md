compatibility: opencode
---
## When to use
- A branch, diff, or completed task needs review.

## Checklist
- What changed and why
- Which files or layers are affected
- Regression risk
- Compatibility risk
- Missing tests or missing verification
- Risky assumptions or edge cases

## Rules
- Prefer concrete findings over generic advice.
- Call out missing evidence, not only code issues.
